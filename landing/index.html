Features

Everything the OCR nikalne ke liye chahiye

Simple, fast, and completely private — TextKaro turns any photo into editable text in seconds.

Add photo any way

Gallery, camera, drag-and-drop, ya paste — koi bhi photo, instantly pick karo. Formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF & more.

English + Urdu OCR

Hindi/English text nikalo, Urdu text nikalo, ya dono ek saath — mixed-language photos bhi sahi se parse hoti hain.

100% on-device

Sab kuch tumhare browser ke andar chalta hai. Photo kabhi upload nahi hoti — koi server, koi storage, koi tracking nahi.

Fully editable text

Extracted text ko directly edit karo — corrections karo, format karo, aur apna final text banao. White on black, clear and crisp.

One-tap copy

Ek click mein poora text clipboard par — paste karo jahan chahe: notes, email, WhatsApp, ya koi bhi app.

Download .txt + counts

Text ko .txt file mein download karo, aur word/character counts live dekh kar — perfect for students, writers, and hard workers.

How it works

Text nikalna ab 3 steps mein

1

Photo add karo

Gallery se choose karo, camera se click karo, drag-and-drop karo, ya sirf paste kardo (Ctrl+V).

2

Text instantly niklega

OCR engine foran kaam karta hai — English, Urdu, ya dono — sab tumhare browser ke andar, bina upload kiye.

3

Edit, copy, download

Text ko edit karo, one-tap copy karo, ya .txt file ke tor par download karo. Done!

Privacy first

Your photo never leaves your device

Most OCR apps bhejte hain tumhari photos apne servers par. TextKaro alag hai — sab processing tumhare browser mein hoti hai.

  • No uploads, ever

    Aapki photo network par kabhi nahi jaati.

  • No sign-up, no data

    Koi account nahi, koi email nahi, koi tracking nahi.

  • Runs in your browser

    Saari OCR processing tumhare browser ke andar hoti hai — kisi server par bhejne ki zaroorat hi nahi.

Device-only processing

Aapki photo, aapka text, aur aapki privacy — sab aapke paas rehta hai. 🔒

No servers No login No ads No trackers
